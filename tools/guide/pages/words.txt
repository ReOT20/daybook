# Lexicon
> Find the hidden word

## How to play

Type a word on the keyboard under the board and press Enter. A physical keyboard works too: type letters, Backspace deletes, Enter submits.

Each letter of a guess is then marked:

- Green: the right letter in the right place.
- Yellow: the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
- Grey: the word has no (more) of that letter.

A letter is marked only as many times as the word holds it, so a second E can be grey while the first is yellow. The keys keep the best mark each letter has had.

A row that isn't a word is refused with "Not in the word list", and doesn't use up a guess. Any real word is allowed, even one the marks have already ruled out, and that can be a good way to test several letters at once.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. A hint that points at a word gives it as a crossword-style clue, and only "Show me" types it.

## Rules

- Find the hidden word before you run out of guesses.
- Every guess must be a real word of the right length.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: Five-letter words, six guesses.
- Hard: Five-letter words, five guesses.
- Expert: Four-letter words, seven guesses. Short words have more look-alikes, so the extra guess is there for a reason.

## Techniques

These are the steps the hints teach. On the board, a green tile has an inner line and a yellow one a corner dot.

### opener | A good opening word

Nothing is known yet, so start with a word of common letters: it cuts the list down fast. The hint gives you one as a clue, and which one changes from board to board. It says nothing about the hidden word.

### best-guess | The word that narrows it most

When the marks force nothing, pick a word that fits them and splits what's left. The hint reads the answer list here, says so, and tells you how far its word narrows things down, given as a clue.

### pinned-letter | A letter pinned to its slot

You know a letter is in the word. Cross off each slot a mark has ruled out for it and each slot already held by a green. If only one slot is left, that's where it goes.

~~~board
L E A N
T E N D
. . . T
tiles: r1c1=out r1c2=right r1c3=out r1c4=near r2c1=near r2c2=right r2c3=right r2c4=out
focus: r2c1
cite: r1c2 r2c1 r2c3
answer: r3c4=T
caption: The rings mark the yellow T and the two greens. T is in the word but not in slot 1, and slots 2 and 3 hold E and N, so T goes in slot 4.
~~~
