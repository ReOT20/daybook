# Mosaic
> Flood the board down to one colour

## How to play

Pick a colour below the board, then tap an area to flood it with that colour. Tapping an area in its own colour does nothing and costs nothing.

An area is squares of one colour joined edge to edge, drawn with a heavy outline. On a keyboard, number keys pick a colour, the arrows move, and Space or Enter pours it.

The limit is the fewest fills the board can be done in, so every fill has to count. One fill off the best line loses the board, and a hint will say which fill lost it and how far back to undo.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". A hint only ever offers a fill that has been checked to still finish inside the limit.

## Rules

- Turn the whole board one colour before the fills run out.
- A fill turns one area a new colour, and it merges with every neighbouring area already in that colour.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: 8x12 grid, 3 colours, 4 moves.
- Hard: 9x14 grid, 4 colours, 5 moves.
- Expert: 10x16 grid, 5 colours, 6 moves.

## Techniques

These are the reasons the hints give, plainest first. There's no answer to deduce here, only a fill to choose, and a hint gives one of these reasons only for a fill that keeps the board winnable. On each board, the tinted area is the one to fill. Each board's colours are this page's, not the game's. Every board here is a real position from the game, and this is the step the hint gives on it.

### finish | The fill that finishes the board

If every other area touches one area, and they're all the same colour, pour that colour in and the board is done.

### count | Counting fills against colours

A fill wipes out a colour only by covering its last patch. So if you have one fill for each colour you must wipe out, every fill has to do exactly that. Look for a colour that's down to one patch.

### centre | Growing from the centre

Grow the most central area: the one with every other area the fewest steps away. Each fill reaches one step further, so the middle gets everywhere soonest.
