# Lamps
> Light every square, no lamp seeing another

## How to play

Tap a square for a cross, a note that no lamp goes there, and tap again to clear it. Drag to lay crosses across squares.

Double-tap or long-press a square for a lamp, and tap a lamp to remove it.

On a keyboard, the arrows move, L or Enter places a lamp, X or Space a cross, and Backspace clears.

Lit squares are tinted. Lamps that light each other, and a number with too many lamps or no longer enough room, turn red about a second later, but nothing stops you placing them.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". Every board has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess to find it.

## Rules

- A lamp lights its own square and its whole row and column, up to a wall or the edge.
- Light every white square. No lamp may light another lamp.
- A number on a wall is how many lamps sit right beside it: above, below, left or right.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: 7x7 grid. About a quarter of the squares are walls on every tier. Needs nothing past the four direct techniques below, the ones up to "the one square that can light another".
- Hard: 10x10 grid. Needs a lamp that would starve a number or leave a square dark, and never a what-if.
- Expert: 12x12 grid. Needs a what-if.

## Techniques

These are the steps the hints teach, simplest first. Try them in this order. On each board, the dark squares are walls, ● is a lamp, a dot is a square a lamp lights, and x is a cross. The tinted squares are where to look, rings mark what the reasoning leans on, and the filled squares are the move. Every board here is a real position from the game, and this is the step the hint gives on it.

### room | A number with just enough room

### what-if | A what-if
