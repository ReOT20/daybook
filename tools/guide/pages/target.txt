# Target
> Six numbers make the target

## How to play

Tap a number, tap + − × ÷, then tap another number. The answer takes the first number's place as a new tile, ready to use in the next step.

Subtract and divide put the bigger tile first whichever you tap first, so there is no order to get wrong.

Undo takes a step back.

On a keyboard, type a tile's number, then + - * /, then another tile's number and Enter. Backspace steps back.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint points at two tiles, and the second says what to do with them and how far that leaves you from the target. It always gives a step on a shortest way from where you stand, the one whose answer lands nearest the target. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me".

Most boards have more than one answer, though Expert's can have just one, and where there's room a longer way round is fine. A hint only calls a step a mistake when no way of combining what's left can reach the target any more, and "Show me" then takes that step back, along with anything after it.

## Rules

- Make a tile equal to the target. Combine two tiles with + − × ÷, and the answer replaces them as a new tile.
- Each number is used once, and you need not use them all.
- Every answer must be a whole number from 1 to 9,999: no negatives, fractions or zero.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: Three large numbers among the six. The large numbers are 25, 50, 75 and 100. The target takes three or four steps at the least.
- Hard: Two large numbers among the six. The target takes four or five steps at the least.
- Expert: One large number, and the answer uses all six. The target takes five steps at the least, and there are only a few ways to make it.
