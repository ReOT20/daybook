# Cage Sudoku
> Sudoku where dashed cages must add up

## How to play

Tap a square, then tap a digit. Tap the digit again to clear it.

Or pick the digit first: with no square selected, tap a digit, then every square it goes in. Tap the selected square to let go of it, and the digit again to finish.

Tap the pencil to take notes. While it is lit, a digit is pencilled small in the square instead of placed, and placing a digit clears it from the notes of the squares it sees and of its cage.

On a keyboard, the arrows move, 1 to 9 place a digit (or a note in pencil mode), and Backspace clears.

Clashing digits, and cages that are over their total or finished wrong, are shown in red, but nothing stops you placing one.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". If a digit you placed is wrong, Hint points at that first. Every board has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess to find it.

## Rules

- Fill every square with a digit from 1 to 9.
- No digit may repeat within a row, a column or a 3x3 box.
- The dashed outlines are cages. The digits in a cage add up to the small number in its corner, and a cage never repeats a digit.
- Any digits you start with are given, and stay put.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: Cages of 2 to 4 squares, some digits given. About 28 cages and 14 digits.
- Hard: Cages up to 5 squares, fewer digits given. About 24 cages and 8 digits.
- Expert: Cages up to 7 squares, no digits given. About 24 cages, and the totals are all you start from.

### forty-five | The 45 rule
